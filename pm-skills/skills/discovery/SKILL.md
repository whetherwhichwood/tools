## What this does

Discovery is where products are made or broken. This skill works in two modes: it helps you **plan** a session (agenda, interview questions) before you run it, and it helps you **synthesize** raw notes into structured findings afterward. It also maps findings onto an Opportunity Solution Tree so you connect a clear outcome to real customer problems before jumping to features.

$ARGUMENTS

*If no input is provided above, ask: "Are you planning a discovery session or summarizing one that's already happened? Then share the project context or raw notes."*

## Two modes

| Mode | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan** | Before the session — need an agenda or interview guide | Session plan + question bank |
| **Synthesize** | After the session — have notes or a transcript | Findings + Opportunity Solution Tree |

If raw notes are provided, go straight to Synthesize. Otherwise ask which mode.

## Mode 1 — Plan

### Discovery Plan

**Project:** [Name] | **Date:** [Session date or TBC] | **Duration:** [e.g. 90 min]
**Facilitator:** [Name] | **Attendees:** [Roles or names]

#### Session goal
[One sentence: what must we know by the end that we don't know now?]

#### Key unknowns going in
1. [Most critical unknown]
2. [Unknown 2]
3. [Unknown 3]

#### Agenda

| Time | Block | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 min | Welcome + context | Align on why we're here |
| 5-20 min | Current state | Understand the problem as it exists today |
| 20-45 min | Pain points + impact | Dig into what's broken and who it hurts |
| 45-65 min | Ideal future state | What does good look like? |
| 65-80 min | Constraints + risks | What could stop us? |
| 80-90 min | Next steps | Who does what before the next session |

*Adjust timing to your duration. For 1:1 interviews, drop the welcome and spend more time on pain points.*

#### Question bank

**Current state:**
- Walk me through what happens today when [the problem occurs].
- How often does this happen? Who is affected?
- What do you do to work around it?

**Pain + impact:**
- What's the biggest frustration with the current situation?
- What happens if this doesn't get fixed? What does it cost the business?
- Who feels this pain most acutely?

**Future state:**
- If this were solved perfectly, what would be different about your day?
- How would you know the solution was working?
- What's the minimum that would make a real difference?

**Constraints:**
- What can't we change, even if we wanted to?
- Has this been tried before? What happened?
- Who needs to approve any change?

**For sponsors only:**
- What does success look like in 12 months?
- What would make you pull the plug on this project?

#### What to capture during the session
- Exact quotes — people's own words reveal what they care about
- Disagreements between attendees — these are hidden risks
- Anything said with strong emotion
- Items deferred with "we'll figure that out later"

#### Opportunity Solution Tree
Connect the desired outcome to problems worth solving, then to possible solutions.

- **Desired outcome:** [A single measurable outcome, e.g. "increase 7-day retention to 40%"]
- **Opportunities** (customer needs/pains, not features — framed as "I struggle to…"):
  - [Opportunity 1] → candidate solutions: [3+ ideas] → experiments: [cheap tests]
  - [Opportunity 2] → …

Rank opportunities by Opportunity Score = Importance × (1 − Satisfaction). Always generate at least 3 solutions per opportunity before choosing — avoid the first-idea trap.
